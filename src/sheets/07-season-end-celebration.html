Season-End Celebration

One page to mark a season of showing up together

How to use this sheet, kindly

Every few months, sit down together (a call counts) and fill one out each. Read yours out loud. Count everything, especially the restarts — a season with six gentle restarts is a season of six gentle restarts, not a failure with a footnote. Then decide together: continue, rest, or begin again with someone new. All three are good answers.
