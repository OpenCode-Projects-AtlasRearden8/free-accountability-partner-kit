Promise-Made-Visible Slips

Small promises, written down, where you can see them

How to use this sheet, kindly

Cut along the dashed borders. One promise per slip — small enough to finish in a week, clear enough that a friend could tell whether it happened. Give one slip to your buddy at each check-in and keep one for yourself. A finished slip gets tossed with a little ceremony; an unfinished one gets rewritten smaller, not repeated louder.
