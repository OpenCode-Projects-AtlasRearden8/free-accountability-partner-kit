Restart Without Shame

For the week everything fell apart (it happens to everyone)

How to use this sheet, kindly

Print this before you need it and keep it somewhere easy. When a week goes sideways, this page replaces the lecture you'd give yourself. Bring it to the check-in instead of an apology. Stopping is a pause, not a verdict.

First, the facts

Missing a week is data, not a character flaw. People who succeed long-term are not people who never stop — they are people who restart often and gently.

The three questions (answer short, be kind)

PromptYour answer
What actually happened this week, plainly?
What got in the way — life, energy, or the promise was too big?
What is the smallest possible restart for tomorrow?

Shrink the restart until it's impossible to fail

  • Not "get back on track" — just open the notebook.
  • Not "exercise again" — just put on the shoes.
  • Not "catch up on everything" — just do today's one thing.

Words to say to your buddy

"I fell off. I'm not quitting — I'm restarting smaller. Here's my tiny restart: name it."

A good buddy hears that as a win, because it is one.
