Long-Distance Postcard Prompts

When your buddy is far away, mail can carry the check-in

How to use this sheet, kindly

For friendships that live across miles or time zones. Once a month, pick one prompt, write it on a real postcard, and mail it. Real mail is slow accountability — the kind that says "I was thinking of you on a Tuesday." Keep the address on the card itself, not stored anywhere online.
