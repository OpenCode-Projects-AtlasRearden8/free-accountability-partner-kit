Find-a-Buddy Pitch Script

A gentle way to ask someone to be your accountability friend

How to use this sheet, kindly

Read this once through, then say it in your own words. The script is a starting point, not a speech. Choose someone who feels safe, not someone who feels impressive. A "no" is a complete answer and a kind one — it protects the friendship.

The ask, in three short pieces

1. Name what you are doing. "I'm trying to build a small habit — name it here — and I do better when I'm not doing it alone."
2. Make the ask small. "Would you be up for a fifteen-minute check-in once a week? Just a call or a text. You wouldn't have to fix anything or keep track of anything."
3. Make it easy to say no. "If it's not your thing, no worries at all — I'd rather know than have you feel stuck."

If they say yes

  • Thank them — then stop talking. Let the yes land.
  • Pick the first time together, today if you can. Vague plans dissolve.
  • Hand them the First-Call Agenda Card so they know it's low-stakes.

If they say no (or "not now")

  • "Thanks for telling me straight — that helps."
  • You asked bravely. The next ask will be easier.
  • Keep this sheet. The right buddy is usually one conversation away.
