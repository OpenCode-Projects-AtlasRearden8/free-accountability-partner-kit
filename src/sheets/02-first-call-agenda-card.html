First-Call Agenda Card

Twenty minutes, three parts, zero pressure

How to use this sheet, kindly

Each of you holds a copy for your very first call. The goal of call one is not progress — it is warmth. If you run out of things to say, that's allowed. Silence between friends is still friendship.

Part 1 — Settle in (5 minutes)

  • How is your week actually going? No editing.
  • What made you say yes to this?

Part 2 — Make it workable (10 minutes)

QuestionMeYou
One small thing I want to be nudged about
How I like to be nudged (text, call, gentle hint)
What I do NOT want asked about
A good day for our weekly check-in

Part 3 — Close warmly (5 minutes)

  • Agree on the next time before hanging up.
  • Say one thing you appreciate about the other person. Out loud.

Our working agreement

We keep what's shared between us. We let each other restart without shame. We cancel honestly and reschedule kindly.
